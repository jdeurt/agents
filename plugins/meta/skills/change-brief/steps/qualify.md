# Qualify unsupported claims

## Purpose

Produce a brief that makes unsupported claims and evidence gaps explicit.

## Inputs

- Candidate: the proposed change paragraph.
- Verdict: the nested review's unsupported result.
- Reasons: the specific unsupported claims and evidence gaps.

## Instructions

Rewrite unsupported factual claims as unverified expectations or remove them when unnecessary. Retain supported claims. Add the review's material limitations. Do not present this revision as independently approved. Block if the reasons are too ambiguous to identify a safe qualification.

## Outputs

- Candidate: the qualified paragraph with uncertainty visible.
- Verdict: unsupported, describing the original candidate's review.
- Reasons: the supplied reasons, with a note that the revised candidate has no second review.
