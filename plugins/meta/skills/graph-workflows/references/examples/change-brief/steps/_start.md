# Establish change inputs

## Purpose

Prepare the declared inputs for an evidence-checked change brief.

## Inputs

- Change facts: a supplied description of the change and its expected observable behavior.
- Evidence notes: supplied checks, observations, and limits relevant to those facts.

## Instructions

Check that both artifacts are present. Block if either is missing. Preserve uncertainty in the supplied facts. Treat absent evidence as a reason to qualify a claim, not as evidence that the claim is false.

## Outputs

- Change facts: the supplied facts with their uncertainty preserved.
- Evidence notes: the supplied observations and limits.
