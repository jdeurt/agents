# Draft the brief

## Purpose

Create a concise candidate change brief for evidence review.

## Inputs

- Change facts: the supplied description and expected behavior.
- Evidence notes: the supplied checks, observations, and limits.

## Instructions

Write one candidate paragraph describing the observable change. Include the evidence and any material limitation. Do not invent checks or claim that untested behavior passed. Preserve unverified expectations as expectations. Return the evidence notes with the candidate as explicit review inputs.

## Outputs

- Candidate: one paragraph describing the change, evidence, and limits.
- Evidence: the supplied evidence notes, unchanged in meaning.
