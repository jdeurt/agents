# Assess clarity

## Purpose

Assess whether a candidate brief makes its change and limitations understandable.

## Inputs

- Candidate: the exact text to assess.

## Instructions

Read the candidate independently. Return one concise observation about its clarity. Identify ambiguous wording if present. Do not assess whether its factual claims are true. Do not change Candidate or write shared files.

## Outputs

- Clarity note: a concise observation about clarity, including any ambiguous wording.
